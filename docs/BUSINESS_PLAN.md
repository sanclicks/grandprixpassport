# 📋 GrandPrixPassport — Business Plan

## Mission Statement

> *The roar of the engines. The smell of the tyres. 20 cars launching off the grid — nothing in sport compares to Formula 1 live. Yet for millions of fans, getting there feels impossible — confusing tickets, wrong grandstands, overpriced packages, and zero guidance. GrandPrixPassport was built for every fan who deserves to be there. We eliminate the complexity, protect you from the pitfalls, and plan every detail of your race weekend — so the only thing you need to feel on race day is the same thing that made you fall in love with F1 in the first place.*

| Pain Point | Impact |
|---|---|

## The Solution — 8 Core Modules

1. **Ticket Intelligence** — Real-time price aggregation + alerts
2. **AI Trip Planner** — Claude-powered end-to-end planning
3. **Grandstand Advisor** — Visual comparison + recommendation
4. **Race Weekend Companion** — Circuit guide + schedule
5. **Travel Entertainment Feed** — F1 content for the journey
6. **Hotel & Travel Hub** — Booking aggregation near circuits
7. **Fan Community** — Connect with fans at same race
8. **Live Race Strategy** — Simplified real-time data
