# 📣 GrandPrixPassport — Marketing Strategy

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Like a knowledgeable F1 fan friend — not corporate.
**Values:** Passionate. Empathetic. Expert. Trustworthy. Exciting.
**Avoid:** Jargon. Condescension. Corporate speak. Hyperbole.

## Social Media Strategy

- Circuit photography (Nikon Z8)
- Grandstand view comparisons
- Race weekend atmosphere shots
- Posting frequency: Daily
- Agent: Marketing Agent + Sandeep's photos

### TikTok (@grandprixpassport)
- "Things I wish I knew before my first F1 race"
- "Best grandstand at COTA for $200"
- Quick F1 tips (30–60 seconds)
- Posting frequency: 3x/week

### X / Twitter (@grandprixpassport)
- Real-time race commentary
- Ticket price alerts
- Strategy commentary during races
- Posting frequency: Multiple times race weekends

### Reddit
- r/formula1 (5.2M members) — helpful content posts
- r/f1travel — direct audience
- r/travel — reach non-F1 travelers
- NO spam — only genuine value

## Email Marketing

### Lead Magnets
1. "F1 Race Weekend Packing List" PDF
2. "Which F1 Race Should You Attend?" quiz
3. Price alert signup (highest converting)
4. "First Timer's Complete F1 Guide" email series

### Email Sequences
1. **Welcome (5 emails):** What is GPP, how it works, first tips
2. **Pre-Race Countdown:** 4 emails in 4 weeks before race
3. **Price Drop Alert:** Triggered by data pipeline
4. **Post-Race:** Recap + next race preview
5. **Annual:** "Plan your 2027 F1 season" (December)
