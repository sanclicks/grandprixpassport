# ⚖️ GrandPrixPassport — Legal & Compliance

## Legal Checklist

### Entity Formation
- [ ] Form LLC (Wyoming recommended — $150, no state income tax)
- [ ] Get EIN from IRS (free at irs.gov)
- [ ] Open business bank account (Mercury.com recommended for startups)
- [ ] Create Operating Agreement

### Intellectual Property
- [ ] File USPTO Trademark — "GrandPrixPassport" ($350 per class)
  - Class 39: Travel arrangement services
  - Class 41: Entertainment/sports information services
  - Apply at: teas.uspto.gov
- [ ] Register copyright for original content (auto-applies but register for enforcement)
- [ ] Document trade secrets (pricing algorithms, data models)

### Insurance
- [ ] E&O Insurance (Errors & Omissions) — ~$500/year
  - Protects from booking errors, wrong information claims
- [ ] General Liability Insurance — ~$400/year
- [ ] Cyber Liability Insurance — ~$800/year
  - Required if storing user payment data

### Privacy & Compliance
- [ ] Draft Privacy Policy (GDPR + CCPA compliant)
- [ ] Draft Terms of Service
- [ ] Cookie consent banner implementation
- [ ] Data Processing Agreement (for EU users)

### Affiliate Compliance
- [ ] FTC Disclosure statement (required for affiliate links)
  - Must disclose affiliate relationships on all content
  - Add to footer: "GrandPrixPassport earns commissions from qualifying purchases"
- [ ] Review StubHub/Vivid Seats affiliate terms

## F1 Trademark Usage Rules

Formula 1® trademarks owned by Formula One Licensing B.V.

### CANNOT USE:
- ❌ "F1" or "Formula 1" in company name
- ❌ Official team logos
- ❌ Ferrari, Mercedes, Red Bull, etc. logos
- ❌ Official F1 imagery without license

### CAN USE:
- ✅ Descriptive references in content ("attending a Formula 1 race")
- ✅ Circuit names (Circuit of the Americas, Miami International Autodrome)
- ✅ "Grand Prix" as generic term
- ✅ Driver names (they are public figures)
- ✅ Fan-created content about F1

## Privacy Policy Key Points

Must include:
1. What data we collect (email, name, location, browsing)
2. How we use it (trip planning, price alerts, personalization)
3. Affiliate disclosure (we earn commissions on purchases)
4. Data retention (how long we keep it)
5. User rights (GDPR: access, deletion, portability)
6. Cookie policy
7. Third party services (AWS, Stripe, affiliate partners)

---

## Terms of Service Key Points

Must include:
1. Service description
2. User eligibility (18+ or 13+ with parental consent)
3. Account responsibility
4. Affiliate disclaimer (prices and availability may change)
5. Limitation of liability
6. Dispute resolution
7. Governing law (New Hampshire, USA)
